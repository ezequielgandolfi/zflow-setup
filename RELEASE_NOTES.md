- Home: set where your home is on the Admin page, using your browser's location or typed coordinates
- Scheduler: start automations at sunrise or sunset, with an offset and the days of the week, and check for day or night with the "Day or night" step
- Weather: show the forecast for your home without typing its coordinates
- Devices: start automations when a property stays at a value for a while, like a door left open
- Devices: get low battery and offline alerts, with a "Devices needing attention" card on the Overview and a blueprint to get notified
